    <h1>Privacy Policy for Tarang - Badminton Booking</h1>
    <p class="last-updated">Last Updated: March 30, 2026</p>

    <p>Welcome to <strong>Tarang - Badminton Booking</strong>. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and delete your information when you use our mobile application.</p>

    <h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
    <p>We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our court booking services:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Personal Information:</strong> Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture (if uploaded) provided during account registration or court booking.</li>
        <li><strong>Authentication Data:</strong> Login credentials, Firebase User IDs, and authentication tokens provided when signing in via Email OTP, Phone OTP, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple.</li>
        <li><strong>Transaction & Booking Details:</strong> Booking history, venue reservations, selected time slots, and payment transaction references.</li>
        <li><strong>Device Identifiers & App Data:</strong> Device push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send booking updates and reminders.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>
    <p>We use the collected data for the following purposes:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>To manage user accounts and authenticate your identity.</li>
        <li>To process and confirm court bookings and reservations.</li>
        <li>To send push notifications regarding booking statuses, OTPs, and updates.</li>
        <li>To prevent fraud, ensure app security, and maintain service reliability.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>3. Mobile Device Permissions</h2>
    <p>Our app may request specific permissions on your mobile device to enable key features:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Photos / Storage / Gallery:</strong> Used to save booking receipts/tickets to your device's gallery and to let you upload a profile picture.</li>
        <li><strong>Camera:</strong> Used solely if you scan QR codes for court check-ins.</li>
        <li><strong>Notifications:</strong> Used to deliver push notifications for booking confirmations, reminders, and account alerts.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>4. Third-Party Services & Payment Processing</h2>
    <p>We work with trusted third-party service providers to power our application:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Google Firebase:</strong> Used for secure user authentication, database storage (Cloud Firestore), file storage (Firebase Storage), push notifications (FCM), and app integrity (Firebase App Check).</li>
        <li><strong>ABA Bank (PayWay):</strong> Payments are processed securely via ABA PayWay. <em>We do not store or process your credit card, debit card, or bank account credentials on our servers.</em> All financial transactions are handled directly by the payment provider.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>5. Data Security</h2>
    <p>All data transmitted between your device, our servers, Firebase, and ABA PayWay is encrypted in transit using industry-standard <strong>HTTPS/TLS protocols</strong>. We implement strict security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.</p>
